# App name shortlist (replacing DuoDialect and Parlomi)

Checked 26 September 2026 (Australia/Sydney). Research only: no purchases,
registrations, sign-ups, messages to owners, or project renaming performed.

## Assessment

This is preliminary screening, not trade mark clearance. None of the six names
below returned an exact-name app or trade mark in the searches listed, but
several have similar names in use, and short coined .com domains are almost all
registered already. Any name taken forward needs a trade mark attorney to assess
similarity and rights in Australia, the US and the UK before a commercial
commitment. Do not describe any of these names as "available", "trademark-safe"
or "clear" on the strength of this document.

Criteria used: short (8 letters or fewer preferred), easy to say in several
languages, suggests conversation, exchange or two-sidedness, does not read as a
dating app, and is not confusingly close to Duolingo, Tandem, HelloTalk, Speaky,
italki, or Parlo/Parlai/Parlami/Parliamo/Parlomo.

## Method and sources

- **Apple:** `https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=<name>&country={au,us}&entity=software&limit=25`.
  Checked titles for the name or near-identical spellings. The API is fuzzy and
  does not show unpublished or reserved names.
- **Google Play:** public search page `https://play.google.com/store/search?q=<name>&c=apps&hl=en&gl=AU`,
  fetched with curl, top ~10 titles read. Best effort only: results depend on
  region and personalisation. None of the six had an exact-title match.
- **Domains:** Verisign RDAP (.com), Google Registry RDAP (.app), auDA RDAP
  (.com.au). A 404 is not a purchase guarantee. Premium or reserved status and
  price were not checked, and no registrar checkout was performed.
- **IP Australia:** quick search `https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search/quick/result?q=<name>`.
  Method confirmed against `Parlo` (6 results, matching the Parlomi report).
- **USPTO:** the tmsearch.uspto.gov backend wordmark query (live and dead). Method
  confirmed against DUOLINGO (19) and TANDEM (492). Exact-word match only.
  Phrase checks: "talk even" 0; "troca" 1 live (STUDIO TROCA); "viceversa" 1
  live and "vice versa" 7 live; "linkus" 2 live.
- **Web:** one web search per name for existing products.

## Candidates screened out

Brainstormed around 90 names. Most short coined names (Konvo, Ambos, Mutuo,
Swapsy, Duetto, Voxa, Kambio, Talkova, Swapio, Lingvo, Troka and around 60 more)
have registered .com domains. That alone did not rule a name out. The following
were dropped for a more specific reason:

- **Konvo:** "Konvo: Learn Languages" is already on the App Store (AU/US).
- **Talkamo:** "Talkamos", a chat app, is on Google Play.
- **Echango:** "Echango" app exists on the App Store.
- **YarnSwap:** exact app on the App Store; "yarn" also reads as knitting.
- **Swapsy:** currency-converter apps of that name.
- **Chinwag:** "Chinwag: Party Games"; .app registered.
- **Ambos:** close to AMBOSS medical apps; .app registered.
- **Mutuo:** means "mortgage/loan" in Italian.
- **Lingamo:** unwanted meaning (*lingam*).
- **Yapswap:** "yap" is slightly pejorative; "swap" results are crypto-heavy.
- **Tongswap, Swaptongue, Tongpal, Mezzotalk, Talkteer, Biloquy:** domains are
  free, but they are too long, awkward, or open to unwanted readings.

## Checks NOT completed

- No UKIPO, EUIPO or WIPO Global Brand Database search, even though the UK is a
  target market.
- No goods/services class analysis, sound-alike analysis or common-law
  (unregistered) rights review. Zero exact hits in IP Australia or USPTO does not
  rule out a conflict with similar marks.
- No social-handle checks (Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Facebook).
- Google Play checked for AU only, and not exhaustively.
- No native-speaker check for unintended meanings beyond the obvious languages
  (for example, Portuguese readings of Trocca, or Slavic surname associations
  for Vicevo).
- No Apple or Google name-reservation status (not publicly visible).

## Decision still required

Pick one or two names for professional clearance. Talkeven has the cleanest
digital footprint but is English-first. Trocca and Vicevo work better across
languages but need a get-/-app .com or the .app domain. Linkua has the best
meaning but should go forward only if an attorney is comfortable with the
existing Linkua language blog.
